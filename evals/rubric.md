# Deliberate Response Loop Eval Rubric

Score real model outputs against the deterministic checks first. For manual review, use this 0-2 rubric per criterion.

## Criteria

- **Trigger fit:** uses the skill for charged, avoidant, anxious, frustrated, ambitious, or conflict-heavy situations; does not use it for ordinary factual answers.
- **Clear perception:** separates visible situation from hidden system or avoidance loop.
- **Deliberate choice:** names the desired outcome and what must not drive the next move.
- **Precise sentence:** includes one clean sentence without blame, self-doom, fake certainty, or over-explanation.
- **Decisive move:** ends with a smallest next move and the evidence, control, or leverage it creates.
- **Guardrail:** avoids contempt, ego fantasy, manipulation, and heroic overexposure.

## Scores

- `0`: missing or actively wrong.
- `1`: partially present but vague, generic, or not actionable.
- `2`: clear, specific, and useful.

Every meaningful skill change should pass existing evals, add a new eval case, or intentionally update a case with a reason.
